## C1 State and creatures
- Save state: Folio (seen/befriended), Party (6), Sanctuary list, items, badges; versioned save with export/import file.
- Procedural creatures: species 1–150 from hash(seed+id). Archetype (quadruped, biped, serpent, avian, floating blob), appendages (horns, tails, wings, petals, fins), type-locked hue ranges, noise patterns (fur, scales, bark). Drawn with the art toolkit, idle bob/blink animation. Generated name from a syllable grammar. Evolution = same seed, complexity 1.0→2.0 (bigger, more appendages, richer colour). Prismatic variant 1/4096 (hue shift + sparkle).
- Roaming wild creatures visible in the overworld: 1–3 per chunk, by biome, time of day and weather. Never random grass pop-ups.

## C2 Sparring
- In-world turn-based spar, no screen change; camera eases closer. "Resolve" instead of HP; at 0 the creature sits and yields, nothing dies. Fleeing always works.
- 5 types: Flora, Aqua, Ignis, Terra, Aero, with an effectiveness loop. Stats: Vigor, Strike, Guard, Haste from the species seed. 4 moves, learned by level from a seeded movepool; same-type bonus; weather and biome boosts; gentle statuses (Sleep, Daze, Rooted).
- XP and levels; auto-win if 10+ levels higher.
- Befriending: a yielded creature can be offered a Resonance Lure (crafted from keepsakes) to join you.

## C3 Party and Sanctuary
- Party leader follows you on a trailing path with smooth steering. Pet/feed by click or tap.
- Sanctuary at world 0,0 (with the Ancient Tree landmark) where all extra creatures roam; UI to swap them in and out. Nicknames.

## C4 World POIs
- Resting Crystals that heal the party, placed deterministically and sparsely. Walking slowly also regenerates Resolve.
- Nomad Camps at biome edges: tents, a crystal, a trader, a move tutor, NPC dialogue that reacts to weather and biome.
- Waystones every ~500 tiles; discover them to fast travel from the map. The map fills in as you explore, with custom pins.

## C5 Traversal abilities (unlocked by badges)
Aqua: glide over water (oceans open up). Flora: clear brambles. Terra: push boulders. Aero: hop 1-tile gaps. Ignis: light up dark caves.

## C6 Caves and Wardens
- Cave entrances lead to a separate darker underground noise map, with a light radius, rare spawns, and a strong "Alpha" creature at the end.
- 8 Wardens beside elemental monuments far from spawn; beating one grants a badge. A rival, The Wanderer, who appears now and then at your top level +1.

## C7 Rare things and atmosphere
4 Apex creatures at the far cardinal edges, each behind a small puzzle; one roaming myth whose position changes with real time; creatures that only appear in rain, snow or night; shooting stars at night; aurora in snow biomes.

## C8 Easter eggs and QoL
- Easter eggs: naming a creature "Null" gives a glitch shader; Up Up Down Down Left Right triggers a meteor shower; a sign after walking 10,000 tiles in one direction ("Turn back, it's just math from here"); a DevTools console ASCII map to a hidden treasure; a hidden tent at -999,-999 with a creator NPC called Yoshik; idle 10 min and your party piles on you to sleep; clicking the same water spot 50 times makes something wave and vanish.
- QoL: 2x speed toggle, colourblind type icons, a No-Combat mode (creatures befriend you without sparring), seed sharing in the menu.

Keep 60fps+ on mobile at native refresh rate; creatures cached to offscreen textures per species. Touch-friendly battle UI at 412x915 and 393x852. Vitest for type matrix, stat generation determinism, evolution, catch logic and save migration.
